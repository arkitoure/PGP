## Summary

This proposal seeks approval to allocate a grant of **1333 SOL** to **Physis Labs** for **V1 Ecosystem Launch P1**, the first phase of the broader Physis V1 launch cycle.

Following the Activation and Ship phases, V1 Ecosystem Launch P1 moves the project into coordinated ecosystem release and early scaling. This phase focuses on delivering the V1 foundation across products and services while increasing production capacity for **SmartSpot One** and **SYNCRAL** units to support onboarding, distribution, and wider ecosystem participation.

---

## Impact

* **Why Now:**

  * Previous Activation and Ship phases established the core products, onboarding pathways, and release infrastructure required for V1.
  * The next step is coordinated ecosystem launch combined with physical infrastructure scaling.

* **Opportunities:**

  * Bring the V1 Physis ecosystem into broader public and community-facing availability.
  * Scale SmartSpot One and SYNCRAL availability alongside increased onboarding demand.
  * Improve continuity between hardware, software, Franca, ASTRALIS, and governance-facing experiences.
  * Establish a stronger operating base for subsequent V1 launch phases.

* **Challenges:**

  * Scaling physical units while coordinating multiple ecosystem releases requires disciplined logistics and production management.
  * Product polish, onboarding, infrastructure, and support must remain synchronized as usage expands.
  * Launch execution must preserve reliability while increasing availability.

* **Future Vision:**

  * Successful completion of V1 Ecosystem Launch P1 establishes the foundation for wider adoption, larger production cycles, and subsequent ecosystem launch phases.

---

## Explanation

* **Implementation Overview:**

  * DAO will allocate **1333 SOL** to Physis Labs to support:

    * V1 ecosystem launch coordination and release execution
    * Scaling production and deployment of **SmartSpot One**
    * Scaling production and deployment of **SYNCRAL** units
    * Procurement, manufacturing, assembly, QA, packaging, and logistics
    * Continued product polish across the V1 ecosystem foundation
    * ASTRALIS onboarding and deployment infrastructure expansion
    * Franca integration and supporting client/API systems
    * Documentation, community onboarding, support, and release communications
    * Marketing and launch activity required to support V1 adoption
    * General operations required to sustain development, production, and launch execution

* **Practical Examples:**

  * Increasing SmartSpot One and SYNCRAL production volumes for new operators and users.
  * Completing final V1 release integrations and onboarding flows.
  * Expanding infrastructure to support larger numbers of active devices and participants.
  * Coordinating product delivery, documentation, support, and launch communication as one release cycle.

* **Addressing Corner Cases:**

  * Production and logistics schedules may be adjusted where component availability or external supplier timelines require it.
  * Any unused funds will be carried forward or returned to Treasury subject to Council review.
