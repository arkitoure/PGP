## Summary

This proposal seeks approval to allocate a continuation grant of **0.3446 zBTC and 277 SOL** from the DAO Reserve Treasury to **Physis Labs** for **V1 Ecosystem Launch P3**.

Building on PGP73, P3 represents the final phase of the V1 Ecosystem Launch. The primary focus is **EU partnership, capital expenditure, marketing, and scaling** through the final stages of public release and into sustained ecosystem growth, with SmartSpot One, ASTRALIS, and the supporting software/platform stack remaining central to delivery.

---

## Impact

- **Why Now:**

  - The final stages of release require coordinated partnership, infrastructure, and market development.
  - Continued funding supports delivery momentum and the capacity needed for adoption beyond launch.

- **Opportunities:**

  - Advance EU partnership and regional ecosystem presence.
  - Expand production and infrastructure capacity through targeted capital expenditure.
  - Increase public adoption of SmartSpot One, ASTRALIS, and related services.

- **Challenges:**

  - Coordinating partnership activity, procurement, marketing, and release readiness.
  - Scaling delivery and support while preserving service reliability.

- **Future Vision:**

  - Complete the V1 launch cycle and establish the partnerships, capacity, and public presence required for sustained ecosystem expansion.

---

## Explanation

- **Implementation Overview:**

  - DAO will allocate **0.3446 zBTC and 277 SOL** to Physis Labs to support:
    - EU partnership development and implementation
    - Capital expenditure on equipment and infrastructure supporting production and network capacity
    - Marketing, product demonstrations, launch communications, and public activation
    - Scaling SmartSpot One production, deployment, and fulfilment
    - ASTRALIS onboarding and supporting software/platform capacity
    - General operations required to sustain release execution and subsequent growth

- **Practical Examples:**

  - Coordinating EU partnership activity around public delivery and regional adoption.
  - Procuring equipment and infrastructure needed to support increasing deployment volumes.
  - Expanding product visibility, onboarding, and operator support as availability grows.

- **Addressing Corner Cases:**

  - Partnership and procurement timelines may adjust as delivery requirements evolve.
  - Capital may be rebalanced within the approved scope as operational priorities change.
  - Any unused funds will be carried forward or returned to Treasury subject to Council review.

---

## Rationale

- **Optimal Design Justification:**

  - PGP69 and PGP73 established the funding scope for launch preparation and public delivery; P3 supports the final release stage and continued scaling.
  - EU partnership and capital expenditure strengthen the operational capacity supporting wider adoption.
  - Coordinated marketing and delivery help convert public awareness into active ecosystem participation.

- **Considered Alternatives:**

  - Smaller phased grants, which could constrain procurement and partnership execution while increasing governance overhead.
  - Deferring expansion until after launch, which could limit delivery capacity and public adoption.

- **Consequences of Inaction:**

  - Partnership activity and capacity expansion could become capital constrained during the final release stage.
  - Limited marketing and operational capacity could slow adoption beyond launch.
