## Summary

This proposal seeks approval to release **649.1K PHY** from the DAO’s Reserve Treasury to a **Founder Vanguard** following a combined contribution of **69.7786 SOL** and **0.34493701 BTC**.

The contribution strengthens and diversifies the DAO Reserve Treasury during the **V1 Ecosystem Launch** phase and extends the Founder Vanguard’s long-term participation in Physis governance.

## Explanation

* **Concept Introduction:**

  * This proposal allocates **649.1K PHY** to a Founder Vanguard following a combined contribution of **69.7786 SOL and 0.34493701 BTC** (Per DAO compatibility protocol, BTC was automatically converted to zBTC upon receipt).
  * The arrangement rewards continued alignment during the V1 Ecosystem Launch phase and incentivizes long-term governance participation.

* **Implementation Overview:**

  * Upon approval, the DAO Reserve Treasury will provide vested **649.1K PHY**.
  * The allocation follows the established Founder Vanguard framework, including the approved premium and vesting protocol.
  * Vesting will follow a **15-month hold followed by a 15-month vest**, for a total **30-month period**.

* **Practical Examples:**

  * The contribution was valued against PHY at the respective times of transfer.
  * Over the vesting period, the Founder Vanguard receives portions of PHY until fully unlocked after 30 months.
  * Released PHY may remain aligned with applicable PHY Rewards and governance participation protocols.

* **Addressing Corner Cases:**

  * Each contribution component retains the reference value established at the time it was received by the DAO Reserve Treasury.
  * The final vesting contract will use the exact calculated allocation of **649,126.97 PHY**.

## Pitfalls

* **Reasons for Hesitation:**

  * The allocation increases the DAO’s total vested PHY obligations.
  * Founder Vanguard allocations must remain consistent with the DAO’s approved framework.

* **Potential Problems:**

  * Shorter vesting conditions could introduce unnecessary market pressure.
  * Long-term alignment remains dependent on continued participation within the DAO ecosystem.

---

## Rationale

* **Optimal Design Justification:**

  * The Founder Vanguard framework rewards direct contribution to the DAO while preserving long-term alignment through vesting.
  * The combined SOL and BTC contribution strengthens and diversifies the Reserve Treasury during the V1 Ecosystem Launch cycle.
  * The established premium recognizes continued Founder Vanguard participation.
  * The **15+15** structure provides an appropriate alignment horizon for this allocation size and current stage of ecosystem maturity.

* **Considered Alternatives:**

  * A smaller allocation without the established Founder Vanguard premium.
  * Shorter vesting terms, which would provide less long-term alignment.
  * Dividing the contribution into separate allocations, adding unnecessary governance overhead.

* **Consequences of Inaction:**

  * The completed contribution would remain without a formally approved corresponding allocation.
  * The DAO would delay recognition of additional capital supporting the V1 Ecosystem Launch phase.
