## Summary

This proposal seeks approval to release **1.224M PHY** from the DAO’s Reserve Treasury to a Founding Partner contributing **752 SOL**. The contribution strengthens the DAO Reserve Treasury during the **V1 Ecosystem Launch** phase and extends the Founding Partner’s long-term participation in Physis governance.

## Explanation

* **Concept Introduction:**

  * This proposal allocates **1.224M PHY** to a Founding Partner following a contribution of **752 SOL** to the DAO Reserve Treasury.
  * The arrangement rewards continued alignment during the V1 Ecosystem Launch phase and incentivizes long-term governance participation.

* **Implementation Overview:**

  * Upon approval, the DAO Reserve Treasury will provide vested **1.224M PHY**.
  * The Founding Partner deposits **752 SOL** into the DAO Reserve Treasury.
  * The allocation follows the established Founder Partner framework, including the approved premium and long-term vesting protocol.
  * Vesting will follow a **15-month hold followed by a 15-month vest**, for a total **30-month period**.

* **Practical Examples:**

  * If PHY’s baseline at the time of transfer is **$0.0606**, this “premium” effectively sets a contribution rate of approximately **$0.04662**.
  * Over the vesting period, the Founding Partner receives portions of PHY until fully unlocked after 30 months.
  * Released PHY may remain aligned with applicable PHY Rewards and governance participation protocols.

* **Addressing Corner Cases:**

  * If the Founding Partner does not deposit the agreed SOL, the DAO will reverse the allocation.
  * The reference value remains established at the time of receipt of SOL into the DAO Reserve Treasury.

---

## Pitfalls

* **Reasons for Hesitation:**

  * A further allocation to an existing Founding Partner may raise questions regarding circulating supply or market impact.
  * Founder Partner premiums must remain consistent with the DAO’s approved tier framework.

* **Potential Problems:**

  * Shorter vesting conditions could introduce unnecessary market pressure.
  * Long-term alignment remains dependent on continued participation within the DAO ecosystem.

---

## Rationale

* **Optimal Design Justification:**

  * The Founder Partner framework rewards significant direct contribution to the DAO while preserving long-term alignment through vesting.
  * **752 SOL** materially strengthens the Reserve Treasury during the V1 Ecosystem Launch cycle.
  * The established premium recognizes the scale and duration of the Founding Partner’s commitment.
  * The **15+15** structure provides continued alignment while reflecting the current stage of ecosystem maturity.

* **Considered Alternatives:**

  * A smaller allocation without the established Founder Partner premium.
  * Shorter vesting terms, which would provide less long-term alignment.
  * Dividing the contribution across multiple smaller arrangements, adding unnecessary governance overhead.

* **Consequences of Inaction:**

  * The DAO would forgo additional Reserve Treasury capacity during the V1 Ecosystem Launch phase.
  * Reduced reserves could constrain future mission-aligned grants and launch execution.
