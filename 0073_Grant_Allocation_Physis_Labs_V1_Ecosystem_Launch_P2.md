## Summary

This proposal seeks approval to allocate a continuation grant of **1000 SOL** to **Physis Labs** for **V1 Ecosystem Launch P2**.

Following the launch foundation established in PGP69, V1 Ecosystem Launch P2 moves Physis into **active public delivery and scale**. The primary focus is expanding SmartSpot One production and availability, strengthening ASTRALIS onboarding, advancing the supporting software/platform stack, and increasing public adoption as the V1 ecosystem moves into users’ hands.

P2 targets a proposed public launch and delivery milestone during the **second week of October 2026** (or earlier), subject to production, logistics, infrastructure, and onboarding readiness. This date is intended as an operational target rather than an unconditional deadline.

## Explanation

* **Implementation Overview:**

  * DAO will allocate **1000 SOL** to Physis Labs to support:

    * Scaling production and deployment of **SmartSpot One**
    * Procurement, manufacturing, assembly, QA, packaging, and fulfillment
    * Production of the first phase lots for **SmartSpot One specification 1 and specification 2**
    * Expansion of **ASTRALIS onboarding** and deployment infrastructure
    * SmartSpot software and platform integration
    * Continued development of APIs, monitoring, update, and recovery systems
    * Franca integration and supporting client/platform capabilities
    * Product documentation, operator support, and public onboarding
    * Network reliability and operational tooling
    * Marketing and public activation supporting V1 availability
    * Supporting SYNCRAL development and deployment where aligned with V1 delivery
    * General operations required to sustain development, production, infrastructure, and launch execution

* **Practical Examples:**

  * Increasing SmartSpot One production volumes for public delivery.
  * Producing and shipping the first phase lots of SmartSpot One specification 1 and specification 2.
  * Reducing manual intervention during ASTRALIS onboarding.
  * Improving provisioning, registration, node status, and operator workflows.
  * Expanding network monitoring and support as active deployments increase.
  * Producing public-facing demonstrations, onboarding materials, product content, and operator communications ahead of the proposed October launch window.
  * Iterating platform and user experience based on live deployment feedback.

* **Addressing Corner Cases:**

  * Production and logistics schedules may adjust where component availability or supplier timelines require it.
  * The second week of October 2026 is a proposed target window and may be revised if readiness requirements are not met.
  * Capital may be rebalanced between production, infrastructure, operations, and marketing as live delivery requirements evolve.
  * Any unused funds will be carried forward or returned to Treasury subject to Council review.

## Rationale

* **Optimal Design Justification:**

  * PGP69 established the V1 launch foundation; P2 deploys capital directly into public delivery and scale.
  * Existing operating obligations have been substantially reduced, allowing a greater proportion of this grant to move directly into production, infrastructure, and adoption.
  * A larger tranche gives Physis Labs the ability to make production and procurement decisions efficiently rather than operating through fragmented micro-grants.
  * SmartSpot One and ASTRALIS now represent the primary path for converting ecosystem development into active public participation.
  * A defined October target creates accountability and coordination without treating the date as a fixed commitment independent of operational readiness.

* **Considered Alternatives:**

  * Smaller phased grants, which would preserve more immediate Treasury liquidity but could constrain production and increase governance overhead.
  * Slower production expansion, which would reduce near-term capital requirements but delay public availability and adoption.
  * Avoiding a target launch window, which would provide greater scheduling flexibility but reduce coordination across production, onboarding, marketing, and public communications.

* **Consequences of Inaction:**

  * SmartSpot availability and public onboarding could become capital constrained during a critical launch window.
  * Production inefficiencies and fragmented procurement could increase costs.
  * Public momentum established during V1 Launch P1 could be weakened by limited availability or onboarding capacity.
  * Without a shared target window, launch-related workstreams may become less coordinated.
